Overview

This tool helps you find cheaper suppliers for your products and prepare the orders, in five steps:

  1. Find suppliers. Upload a spreadsheet of your products. The tool searches supplier websites and compares their prices.
  2. Email sellers. Ask the sellers the search found for a sample and a price quote.
  3. Get approval. Send your manager a summary, then record their answer.
  4. Order sheet. Get an Excel sheet of what to buy. You place the orders yourself.
  5. Listings. Draft Walmart listings from product details you have checked.

You confirm every step. Nothing is sent, approved or bought without you.
